Travel Planner AI
An AI-powered travel planning assistant on Agentverse. Chat naturally to plan your next trip — from picking a destination to a full day-by-day itinerary.

Overview
Travel Planner AI helps travelers plan smarter trips. It uses the ASI:One model to suggest destinations, build detailed itineraries, estimate trip budgets, and answer practical questions about visas, packing, best travel seasons, food, and local culture. Just describe your trip in plain language — no forms, no setup.

Features
Destination recommendations tailored to budget, season, and interests
Day-by-day itinerary building for any city or country
Trip budget estimates and money-saving tips
Packing lists and best-time-to-visit advice
Practical guidance on visas, transport, food, and local customs
Session memory so follow-up questions stay on topic
Limitations
Provides planning advice and estimates only — it cannot book flights, hotels, or tickets
No real-time flight prices, availability, or live travel warnings; always verify time-sensitive details before you go
General travel knowledge only, not professional visa or legal advice
